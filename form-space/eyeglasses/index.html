What this is

Every possible pair of eyeglasses this page can say, written as choices and the rules about which choices fit together. Every valid set of choices has a whole number. Nothing is stored: a pair is drawn when you land on its number.

Nothing here is ranked or filtered. Odd pairs are part of the space. Whether a pair can be made, what it costs and whether it is good are left to you.

  • Choices are sorted by the distance at which you notice them, from the silhouette across the room down to the sound of the hinge.
  • new marks an option not in current practice. extreme marks one outside the ordinary range. Both are from memory, unverified.
  • The front view shows the lenses indoors, the worn view outdoors. Dashed lines in the top view show parts that move.
  • Kept pairs and holds are saved on this device only.

Total:

Any two axes as a grid. The rest stay as they are. Tap a cell to go there.

A language is one value for each choice that carries character: outline, weight, marks, colour, material, surface, finish. Roll one and it is held, so Anywhere and Drift change only the rest.

  1. A sharp outline takes sharp marks and crisp edges. A soft outline takes round marks and rounded edges.
  2. Rim, temple and corner weight move together.
  3. Thin rims take metal or carbon. Heavier rims take sheet and moulded materials.
  4. Warm colours take warm metal. Cool colours take cool metal.
  5. One material, one finish and one pattern run through the whole pair.
